# A Tribute to Barbara DeVore

A tribute page honoring my grandmother, Barbara DeVore: her story, her faith,
and her life in her own words.

## About the Project

This began as a freeCodeCamp Tribute Page project and grew into a fully
redesigned, interactive site.

I built this page to celebrate the woman who inspired my work ethic and love
for family. The written sections come from Grandma's own answers to questions
I asked her, and the design uses her favorite colors: greens, teals, and blues.

## Features

- **Responsive layout:** the photo and her story wrap side by side on desktop
  and stack neatly on mobile
- **Auto-scrolling photo gallery:** pauses on hover, with arrow buttons for
  manual scrolling and a seamless infinite loop
- **Gallery viewer:** click any photo to open an enlarged view with a
  scrollable thumbnail grid and previous/next navigation (keyboard arrows
  and Esc supported)
- **Animated wave borders:** layered SVG waves with an ombre effect and a gold
  accent line around the scripture quotes
- **Color-cycling word tags:** each tag shifts through Grandma's favorite colors
  at its own pace
- **Accessibility:** alt text on every photo, keyboard support, and reduced-motion
  support for visitors who turn off animations
- **Optimized images:** resized for fast loading on desktop and mobile

## Built With

- HTML5
- CSS3 (Grid, Flexbox, custom properties, keyframe animations)
- Vanilla JavaScript (no frameworks)
- Google Fonts: Playfair Display & Lora
- Hosted on GitHub Pages
